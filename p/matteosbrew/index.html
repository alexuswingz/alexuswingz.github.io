What I built

  • Full-bleed video hero, animated sections for the story, menu preview, events, packages and location, all tuned for phones first.
  • Digital menu page: 26 drinks across Classics, Signature, Specialty, Milk, Matcha and Chocolate with instant search and category filters.
  • QR-code section generates scannable codes that drop customers straight into the menu from the counter or table.
  • Ships as a static build, so hosting costs nothing and it loads instantly.
