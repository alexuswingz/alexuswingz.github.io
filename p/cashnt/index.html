What I built

  • "Safe to spend today" engine computes one daily number from balances, bills, paydays and savings goals.
  • AI money coach: ask "can I afford this?" in plain language and get a plan generated from your actual financial data. Production LLM integration, not a demo.
  • Net worth across cash, banks, e-wallets (GCash, Maya), investments and crypto; recurring bills and payday detection; envelope-style trip budgets; group pots with automatic who-owes-who settlement.
  • Privacy as a feature: local-first guest mode, Face ID lock, privacy masking, in-app account deletion, published privacy policy. Passed App Store review on the first submission.
  • Custom sync API on Railway: Apple / Google / email sign-in, cross-device snapshot sync, group spend endpoints.
  • Runs the whole product operation solo: App Store optimization, release management (v1.1 shipped within days of launch), pricing and roadmap.
