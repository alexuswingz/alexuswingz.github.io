What I built

  • Streaming chat on the Next.js App Router with a system prompt engineered for empathy: acknowledge the person's situation, tie answers to their specific case, explain statutes simply, always finish with actionable steps.
  • Covers the Constitution, Civil Code, Revised Penal Code, Labor Code, Family Code, property, tax, corporate and procedural law, with a clear "not legal advice" boundary.
  • Per-user conversation history persisted in PostgreSQL (conversations and messages APIs, auto-initialised schema), plus image upload for document analysis.
  • Mobile-first glass UI with popular-question shortcuts and a PWA manifest so it installs like a native app.
